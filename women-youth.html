Women & Youth Empowerment

Two groups the standard workday leaves out.

Rigid, long hours are a well-documented barrier to women's workforce participation, and a bottleneck on entry-level opportunity for youth. Both are central to why we're pushing for reform.

Women's workforce participation

Household responsibilities disproportionately fall on women, and an 8–9 hour (plus commute) workday often makes formal employment incompatible with them. A predictable 6-hour shift is one concrete lever to change that math.

  • Advocacy for shift structures that fit around caregiving
  • Support connecting women to Suvidha and jobs listings
  • Community spaces to discuss workplace concerns openly

Youth employment

India's workforce skews young. A four-shift employment model can open more entry points for first-time jobseekers, while mentorship connects them with people who've already navigated that path.

  • Entry-level listings through our Jobs platform
  • One-to-one mentorship pairing
  • Skills pathways built for a first job, not just any job
