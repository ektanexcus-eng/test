Join Ekta NexCus
Vision India 2047 · Labour Reform Initiative

Reimagining the future of work and community.

Ekta NexCus works toward a future where people have real opportunities to work, learn, connect, contribute — and still have time left over for family and community. We build that future through policy advocacy, worker-facing platforms, and grassroots organising across Maharashtra.

Why we exist

A simple idea: work should fit inside a life, not replace it.

Across Maharashtra's organised and unorganised sectors alike, long hours are often treated as the price of a livelihood. Ekta NexCus starts from a different premise — that dignity, productivity, family time and community participation can all coexist, if working hours are designed with people in mind.

Policy advocacy

We research, draft and publicly advocate for labour reform — anchored by the 6-Hour Work Policy proposal — grounded in constitutional principles of dignity and livelihood.

Worker platforms

Suvidha and our jobs listings connect unorganised-sector workers in the Kolhapur–Sangli–Satara–Pune–Solapur corridor with services, transport and opportunity.

Community organising

Volunteers, mentors, youth and local partners carry this work forward on the ground, in Marathi, Hindi and English.

Our central proposal

The 6-Hour Work Policy

A proposed shift from the standard 8–9 hour workday to a 6-hour day — same dignity and productivity, structured through a four-shift model, with the hours returned to workers spent on family, health, learning and community.

Read the full policy case
01

Family time

Two extra hours a day returned to parenting, elder care and household life.

02

Mental wellbeing

Shorter, well-structured shifts to ease the burnout common in long-hour work.

03

Youth employment

A four-shift model opens more entry points into formal and informal work.

04

Women's participation

Shorter days lower one real barrier to women entering and staying in the workforce.

A note on the 6-Hour Work Policy: this is Ekta NexCus's own advocacy proposal and awareness initiative — a vision for what Indian labour policy could become. It is not an enacted law, government scheme, or official policy of any state or central authority.
One-stop worker platform

Suvidha

Suvidha is Ekta NexCus's platform for the practical, everyday needs of workers and communities — starting with a zero-commission, self-registered ride-sharing network piloted across Kolhapur district, and growing toward a broader hub for jobs, services and support.

Transport

Work

A growing bridge between local employers and jobseekers.

Support

Community

Built by and for the corridor's own residents.
