One-stop worker platform

Suvidha

A self-sustaining platform built for the practical, everyday needs of workers and communities across the Kolhapur–Pune corridor — starting with transport, and growing from there.

Live pilot

Zero-commission ride-sharing

Suvidha's transport layer is a self-sustaining, zero-commission ride-sharing network piloted in Kolhapur district. Drivers self-register directly on the platform, with no middleman commission cutting into their earnings, covering all 12 talukas within roughly a 60 km radius.

  • Self-registration for drivers, verified before activation
  • No commission taken from driver earnings
  • Coverage across all 12 talukas of Kolhapur district
Register interest as a driver

How it works

  1. Drivers register with basic details and vehicle information.
  2. Requests connect drivers directly with riders in their area.
  3. Fares go to the driver — Suvidha takes no cut.

Booking-flow details and a rider-facing app screen will be added here once finalised.
