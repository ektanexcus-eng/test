Social Impact

Impact areas, reported honestly.

We report what we're working on and how it's meant to connect to broader development goals — without inflating numbers we can't verify.

Family & wellbeing

Time returned to households through shorter, more predictable working hours.

Decent work

Advocating for working conditions that treat time as valuable, not just labour as a commodity.

Reduced inequality

Widening access to formal work for women, youth and the unorganised sector.

Sustainable mobility

Suvidha's shared-ride model reduces per-trip transport costs and vehicle load in the corridor.

Quality education

Directing freed-up time toward skills and learning, not just leisure.

Partnerships

Working with community groups, employers and eventually CSR/NGO partners toward shared goals.

Alignment

How this connects to the UN Sustainable Development Goals

Our work touches several SDGs directionally. This is a statement of alignment with our own goals, not a claim of formal UN endorsement or certification.

SDG 1 · No Poverty SDG 3 · Good Health & Wellbeing SDG 4 · Quality Education SDG 5 · Gender Equality SDG 8 · Decent Work & Economic Growth SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities SDG 11 · Sustainable Communities SDG 17 · Partnerships for the Goals
Impact metrics (people reached, drivers registered, jobs matched, etc.) will be published here once we have verified figures to report. We won't estimate or round up in the meantime.

Ekta NexCus is a Maharashtra-based labour reform initiative working toward a Vision India 2047 where dignity, family time and fair work can coexist. The 6-Hour Work Policy is an advocacy proposal, not an enacted law.
