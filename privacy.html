Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Ekta NexCus ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why, and how it's handled. We aim to follow the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

When you use a form on this site (volunteer, jobseeker, mentorship, partner, event, or contact forms), we collect what you submit — typically your name, phone number, email, city, and any message or notes you add. We do not collect payment information through this site.

How we use it

We use submitted information solely to respond to your enquiry, match you with relevant opportunities (jobs, mentorship, volunteering), or keep you informed about Ekta NexCus programmes you've opted into. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & local storage

This site uses minimal local browser storage (such as remembering your language preference) and does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, request corrections, or ask us to delete it, by writing to ektanexcus@gmail.com.

Grievance officer

Grievance officer name and contact details to be added here before public launch, as required for compliance.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform grows. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Ekta NexCus is a Maharashtra-based labour reform initiative working toward a Vision India 2047 where dignity, family time and fair work can coexist. The 6-Hour Work Policy is an advocacy proposal, not an enacted law.
