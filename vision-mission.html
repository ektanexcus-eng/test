Vision India 2047

A country where work makes room for life.

Our vision and mission sit inside a longer horizon — Vision India 2047 — a century-of-independence framework for what fair, humane work could look like.

Our vision

An India where every worker — in a factory, a farm, a shop, or a gig platform — has enough working hours to earn a living with dignity, and enough hours left over to raise a family, stay healthy, keep learning, and take part in their community.

Our mission

To make the case for a 6-hour workday through honest research and public advocacy, and to prove the idea is workable by building platforms — like Suvidha — that already serve workers better today.

How the vision breaks down

Four pillars

01

Reform the workday

Advocate for the 6-Hour Work Policy as a constitutional-dignity issue, not a productivity slogan.

02

Serve workers directly

Build practical tools — Suvidha, job listings, skills resources — that help today, policy or not.

03

Widen participation

Make room for women and youth who are pushed out of the workforce by inflexible hours.

04

Organise community

Build local, trilingual networks of volunteers, mentors and partners who carry this work forward.

The roadmap

From 2027 to 2047

A directional roadmap, not a fixed promise — it will be revised as programmes are tested on the ground.

2026–2027

Foundation

Grow the Suvidha pilot, formalise the 6-Hour Work Policy proposal, and build the volunteer and mentor base across the corridor.

2028–2032

Proof of concept

Work with willing employers and local bodies to pilot shorter-shift models and publish honest, verified outcomes.

2033–2040

Wider advocacy

Take the evidence base to policymakers, industry bodies and civil society at a state and national level.

2041–2047

Vision India 2047

Contribute to a national conversation about work-life balance in step with India's centenary of independence.

Ekta NexCus is a Maharashtra-based labour reform initiative working toward a Vision India 2047 where dignity, family time and fair work can coexist. The 6-Hour Work Policy is an advocacy proposal, not an enacted law.
